--- Introduction and overview ---

Before we begin, I want to give you an overview of each stage of the process so you know what to expect
and what we're aiming for, as well as give you some useful troubleshooting tips.
In the first section, we'll be creating a procedural walk system for this Scorpion character model.
Our first step will be to calculate the speed of the character.
This will allow us to predict the motion of the scorpion and to control variables such as the leg lift
height based on the movement speed.
To calculate this, we will save the previous position of the character and compare it to the current
position to see how far it is moved in the time that has passed to calculate its movement speed per
second.
Next, we'll loop through all six of the feet bonds of the character, and use the full body IK solver
to allow us to move these bonds to the target points that we specify.
And the solver will adjust the body and limbs of the character in order to reach these target points.
Using this, we will first lock all of the feet in place so that when the actor moves, the character
stays locked in place.
We will then create a timer, which will act as a cycle to decide when to lock and unlock the feet from
their position in the world.
We will offset the timing of each of the feet, so that they do not all move in sync, and instead move
one at a time.
We do this by offsetting the cycle time by a different percentage for each individual foot.
After this, we want to incorporate the movement speed we have calculated to allow the character to
anticipate where it will move to in the future, so that it can step forwards in the direction of movement.
Next, we will make the legs lift in an arc whilst taking a step to create more realistic looking movements,
and we'll use sphere tracers to detect the floor height so that the landing spots can be adjusted to
make the character walk over obstacles.
And finally, we will make some overall tweaks to the animation, including adding additional body motions,
adjusting the variables, and making improvements to the traces so that the character can move through
a variety of environments.
After completing this section, you'll be shown how to port your animation system to another character,
and then we'll move on to looking at more features of Control Rig and some use case examples, which
can be combined with regular Keyframed animations.
And lastly, you will be challenged to create a turning place animation system of your own.
Before being shown my process of figuring out how to approach and implement the system.
The course starts off very slow as we gradually introduce concepts.
So if you are experience with Unreal Engine or animation, you may need to speed up some of the earlier
videos, but I would recommend not skipping through any of them because we will be continually building
on previous sections in the later videos.

--- Basic tips & troubleshooting ---

Whilst working through this course, you will likely run into some crashes, so it's important to save
frequently and ideally create backups of what you're working on.
A useful tip to solve many issues that may arise is to close, control, rig and reopen it, or to close
and reopen the editor.
This is particularly useful in cases where you are getting errors which don't make sense often when
dealing with arrays, where it will tell you that an array you're trying to use is out of bounds.
First, try clicking compile a few times to see if that fixes it and if not, reopen control rig.
For more general issues that you might encounter.
It can be very useful to create print nodes, to print information, to make sure what you think is
happening is what's actually happening.
And if you have errors which seem out of place and don't make any sense, sometimes this may be because
of a problem that has come earlier, rather than where it suggests the error is.
In this case, try cutting out different parts of the logic in control rig to isolate the real cause
of the problem.
If you're still having an issue and you have looked over previous videos again to try to correct any
errors, take a look to see if anyone else has had the same problem in the question section of the video.
If the problem hasn't already been asked and solved, post your own question and I will respond.
Or I can also be contacted on the discord server.
When you're ready to begin, download, extract and open the project file from the course resources
and we will get started.
