Here are 6 pre-built deck lists designed specifically to test the core mechanics, color interactions, and balance thresholds of **Aegis Breach**.

Each deck highlights a unique strategic archetype, stays within your deckbuilding guidelines (50-card Main Deck, 3-copy cap, matching Leader color identities), and utilizes the **Inverse Rank-Based Stat Matrix** and **Dynamic Cost-to-Impact Scaling**.
