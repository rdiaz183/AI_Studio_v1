Naruto Card Game Core RulesDeck Construction: A standard deck consists of 51 total cards—1 Leader card, 5 Chakra cards, 1 Summon card, and a 50-card main deck containing character and EX character cards matching your leader's color. [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQh73mmHrYI&t=16), [2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dlmaoU2OBpM)Resource Management: Summon cards are tapped to play characters, while Chakra cards are flipped to pay for skills or played face-down as support. [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BD65cGfy1nU&t=39), [2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dlmaoU2OBpM)Turn Structure: Turns start with a refresh phase to untap resources, followed by drawing two cards (the first player draws only one on turn one). [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BD65cGfy1nU&t=39), [2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dlmaoU2OBpM)Win Condition: The game concludes when an opponent's Leader health pool drops to zero. You can check official updates and documentation via the Official Rulebook for NARUTO Mythos TCG.
