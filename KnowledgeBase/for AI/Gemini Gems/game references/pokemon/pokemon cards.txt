1. Pokémon CardsPokémon cards are the main fighters in your deck, characterized by their elemental type, evolution stage, and special rule boxes.Elemental Types: There are 11 core types including Grass, Fire, Water, Lightning, Psychic, Fighting, Colorless (Normal), Darkness, Metal, Dragon, and the previously featured Fairy type.Evolution Stages:Basic Pokémon: Can be placed directly from your hand onto the battlefield.Stage 1 & Stage 2 Pokémon: Evolve sequentially from pre-existing Pokémon on the field to grant higher HP and stronger attacks.Special Mechanic Variations (Rule Box Cards):EX / GX / ex: High-HP variants that yield two (or more) Prize cards to your opponent when knocked out. GX cards include a once-per-game GX attack.V / VMAX / VSTAR: Sword & Shield era cards where V cards can evolve into massive VMAX powerhouses or VSTAR variants featuring once-per-game VSTAR powers.Mega Evolution: Evolve from specific Pokémon EX, offering game-altering stats.2. Trainer CardsTrainer cards represent items, supporters, and environments used to manipulate the game state during your turn.Items: Played in any quantity during your turn to assist you (e.g., drawing cards, healing).Pokémon Tools: A sub-category of Item attached to individual Pokémon to grant passive bonuses; a Pokémon can hold only one tool.Supporters: Powerful effects limited to one per turn (e.g., Professor's Research to refresh your hand).Stadiums: Field-wide persistent cards that affect both players until replaced by a new Stadium card.ACE SPEC: Ultra-powerful cards restricted to a single copy per entire deck.3. Energy CardsEnergy cards are attached to your Pokémon to pay the energy costs required to execute attacks.Basic Energy: Provide a single type of corresponding elemental energy (Grass, Fire, Water, etc.) and have no limit in a deck.Special Energy: Provide unique bonus effects or multiple types of energy simultaneously while fulfilling attack costs.
