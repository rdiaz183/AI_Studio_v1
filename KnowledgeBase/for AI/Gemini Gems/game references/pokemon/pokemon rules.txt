Deck Building RulesDeck Size: Exactly 60 cards—no more, no less.Card Limit: Maximum of four copies of a card with the exact same name (except for basic energy cards).Basic Pokémon: Your deck must include at least one Basic Pokémon.Game SetupFlip a coin to see who goes first.Shuffle decks and draw a starting hand of seven cards.Place an Active Pokémon: Choose a Basic Pokémon from your hand and place it face down in the Active Spot.Build your Bench: Put up to five additional Basic Pokémon face down on your Bench.Set Prize Cards: Take the top six cards of your deck and set them aside face down as Prize cards.Reveal: Turn all active and benched Pokémon face up to begin.Structure of a TurnAt the start of your turn, you must draw one card from your deck. During your turn, you can perform these actions in any order:Put Basic Pokémon from your hand onto your Bench (up to five total).Evolve your Pokémon (if they have been in play since the previous turn).Attach one Energy card from your hand to one of your Pokémon.Play Trainer cards (any number of Item cards, but only one Supporter card per turn).Use Abilities.Retreat your Active Pokémon to the Bench by paying its retreat cost in energy.Attack with your Active Pokémon (ending your turn). Note: The player who goes first cannot attack on their very first turn.Winning the GameYou win the game if you do any of the following:Take all of your Prize cards (by knocking out opponent Pokémon).Knock out all of your opponent's Pokémon currently in play.Your opponent has no cards left to draw at the start of their turn.
