The One Piece Card Game is a head-to-head trading card game where two players use a Leader, a 50-card main deck, and a 10-card DON!! deck to reduce the opponent's life to zero.Deck Building RulesLeader Card: Pick one Leader card with a specific color and life value.Main Deck: Exactly 50 cards. All cards must share at least one color with your chosen Leader.Copy Limit: Maximum of four copies of any uniquely numbered card.DON!! Deck: Exactly 10 DON!! cards used as resource currency.SetupPlace your shuffled 50-card deck and 10-card DON!! deck in their zones.Place your Leader face-up.Decide who goes first using rock-paper-scissors.Draw 5 starting cards. Each player can do a one-time mulligan to shuffle and redraw 5 cards.Take cards from the top of your deck equal to your Leader's life value and place them face-down in your Life area.Turn StructureEach turn follows a strict phase order:Refresh Phase: Set all your rested (tapped) cards and DON!! cards back to active (untapped) status.Draw Phase: Draw 1 card from your main deck (the player going first skips the draw on turn one).DON!! Phase: Add 2 active DON!! cards from your DON!! deck into your Cost Area (player one only gets 1 DON!! on turn one). The cap is 10 DON!! in play.Main Phase: Spend active DON!! to play Character, Event, or Stage cards, attach DON!! to boost power, or declare attacks.End Phase: Temporary "until end of turn" effects wear off, and the turn passes.Combat and WinningTargets: You can attack the opposing Leader or any rested Character. Newly played characters cannot attack on the turn they enter the field.Counter Step: The defender can discard cards from their hand with a [Counter] value or play Event counters to boost their power. Ties go to the attacker.
