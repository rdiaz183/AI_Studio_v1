The One Piece Card Game features five primary card types and a distinct combat and resource system.Card TypesLeader Cards: The commander of your deck that stays on the field face-up the entire game. It sets your deck's color identity, dictates your starting Life total (usually between 2 and 5), and can attack or use special abilities.Character Cards: The crew members and foes from the series that form the bulk of your 50-card main deck. They are played onto the field (up to 5 max) to attack opponents, block incoming attacks, and trigger special effects.Event Cards: One-time action cards played from your hand by paying their cost. They resolve an immediate effect (such as removing an opponent's card or boosting power) and then go straight to the trash. Some have "Counter" values or specific timing to disrupt enemy turns.Stage Cards: Ongoing battlefield support cards. You can only have one Stage card active on your field at a time, and they provide continuous passive bonuses to your side.DON!! Cards: The game's unique resource engine kept in a separate 10-card DON!! deck. You add DON!! to your cost area each turn to pay for playing characters, events, and stages, or you can attach them actively to characters and leaders to boost their attack power.Core Gameplay MechanicsRested vs. Active States: Cards start upright (active) and turn sideways (rested) when they attack, use specific abilities, or are tapped to pay for costs. Rested cards cannot block or attack until they refresh at the start of your turn.Counter Step & Defense: When your leader or character is attacked, you can defend from hand by discarding cards with a printed "Counter" value (+1000 or +2000 power) or by playing active Event Counter cards to raise your total defense power above the incoming attack.Trigger Effects: A special mechanic found at the bottom of certain cards. When you take damage and reveal a Life card, if that card has a "Trigger" keyword, you can activate its effect immediately for free before adding it to your hand.Attributes: Characters have combat attributes like Slash, Strike, Ranged, Wisdom, or Special. While mostly thematic, many card effects interact explicitly with specific attributes for tactical synergy.
