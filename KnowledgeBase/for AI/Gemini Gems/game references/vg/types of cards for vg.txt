Types of CardsUnit Cards: The primary cards used to fight. They have a Grade (power level from 0 to 4+), Power (offensive strength), Shield (defensive value when guarding), and Critical (damage dealt on a successful hit).Vanguard (VG): The main unit representing you on the center circle.Rear-guard (RG): Units supporting your vanguard from the side circles.Trigger Units: Specialized Grade 0, 1, or Heal units that activate powerful bonus effects (Critical, Heal, Draw, Front, or Over triggers) when revealed during a drive or damage check.Order Cards: Non-unit spell or utility cards played from your hand into the Order Zone for specific effects.Normal Orders: Standard spells that resolve and go to the drop zone.Set Orders: Cards that stay in the Order Zone to provide continuous or recurring effects (such as Prison, World, or Songs).Crest Cards / Ride Deck Crests: Special non-playable helper cards used in modern formats (like the D-Series) to track rules, such as the Energy Generator.Core MechanicsThe Ride Deck: A separate 4-card deck (Grades 0 to 3) pre-selected before the game, allowing you to reliably "ride" (evolve) your Vanguard a grade higher each turn.Counter Blast (CB): A mechanic where you flip face-down a designated number of cards in your Damage Zone to pay the cost for special unit skills.Soul Blast (SB): A mechanic where you send a designated number of cards from underneath your Vanguard (the Soul) into the Drop Zone to pay skill costs.Drive Checks & Damage Checks: When your Vanguard attacks, you reveal the top card of your deck (Drive Check) to check for triggers and add it to your hand. When you take damage, you do the same (Damage Check), placing the card into your Damage Zone.Perfect Guard (PG): A vital defensive staple ability allowing you to discard a card to completely negate an incoming attack against your Vanguard.Persona Ride: Triggered by riding an exact duplicate copy of your Grade 3 Vanguard, giving your entire front row +10000 power and drawing you cards for that turn.
