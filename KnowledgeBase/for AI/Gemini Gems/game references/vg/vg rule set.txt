Deck ConstructionMain Deck: Consists of 50 cards from a single chosen nation (in the Standard format).Ride Deck: A separate 5-card deck containing specific grades (0 through 3) used to sequentially upgrade your vanguard during the fight.Triggers: Exactly 16 trigger units total, including up to 1 Over Trigger, 4 Heal Triggers, and up to 8 of other trigger types (Critical, Draw, Front).Card Limit: Up to 4 copies of a card with the same name.Field and SetupVanguard Circle: Where your main leader unit sits. Vanguards are permanent unless ridden or replaced.Rear-Guard Circles: Up to five spaces for supporting units. Rear-guards can be destroyed (retired) by opponent attacks or card skills.Guardian Circle: Where units from your hand are placed to intercept and block incoming attacks.Damage Zone: Where cards go when an attack hits your vanguard; reaching 6 damage means a loss.Basic Turn StructureStand Phase: Rested (tapped) cards on your field stand back up.Draw Phase: Draw one card from your deck.Ride Phase: Ride a unit from your ride deck that is equal to or one grade higher than your current vanguard.Main Phase: Call rear-guards from your hand to the field, use abilities, or play orders.Battle Phase: Declare attacks with your vanguard and rear-guards against the opposing vanguard or rear-guards. The opponent can guard using cards from their hand. Reveal trigger checks if attacks hit or during drives.End Phase: Turn ends and control passes to the opponent
