Main Card TypesDigimon Cards: Creatures featuring a level (Lv. 3 to 7), Digital Power (DP), and a memory cost used to attack opponents and defend your security stack.Digi-Egg Cards (Digitama): Level 2 baby Digimon kept in a separate 1–5 card Digi-Egg deck with a unique card back; they hatch and grow in the breeding area.Tamer Cards: Human partners placed in the battle area that cannot attack, but provide permanent, stackable passive effects that alter game rules or boost your monsters.Option Cards: One-use spell or action cards played during your main phase (provided you control a matching color on the field) that trigger immediate tactical effects before going to the trash.Dual Cards: Special modern hybrid cards that act as both Digimon and Option cards simultaneously.Core MechanicsMemory Gauge: A shared resource tracker running from -10 to +10. Playing cards or digivolving costs memory, passing the turn to your opponent when the marker moves to their side (0 or higher).Digivolution: Evolving an active or breeding Digimon into a higher-level card of the correct color by paying a reduced memory cost, which draws you a card and stacks effects.Security Stack: Each player's 5-card life total/defense wall. When an opponent attacks directly, the top security card is revealed to fight or trigger a free security effect.Advanced Mechanics:DNA Digivolution (Jogress): Combining two Digimon on the field into one higher-level Digimon.DigiXros: Placing specific material cards under a new Digimon from hand or field to reduce its play cost.ACE / Blast Evolution: Digivolving for free on your opponent's turn in response to an attack, balanced by a heavy <Overflow> memory penalty if the card leaves the field.
