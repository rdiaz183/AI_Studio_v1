Deck ConstructionMain Deck: Exactly 50 cards, containing Digimon, Tamer, and Option cards. Up to 4 copies of the same card number are allowed.Digi-Egg Deck (Digitama): 0 to 5 Digi-Egg cards, with a limit of 4 copies per card number.Setup and Game StartShuffle your main deck and place it in the deck zone.Shuffle your Digi-Egg deck and place it in the breeding zone.Determine who goes first (via rock-paper-scissors or a coin flip).Draw 5 cards for your starting hand.Place the top 5 cards of your main deck face-down into your security area without looking.Set the shared memory gauge counter to 0.Turn PhasesA player's turn consists of four sequential phases:Active Phase: Unsuspend (switch from rest/horizontal mode back to active/vertical mode) all your suspended cards in your battle area.Draw Phase: Draw 1 card from your main deck (the very first player on turn one does not draw).Raising Phase: Hatch a Digi-Egg into the breeding area or move an adult-level (Lv. 3 or higher) Digimon from the breeding area to the battle field.Main Phase: Play cards, digivolve Digimon, use abilities, or declare attacks in any order by spending memory.The Memory Gauge SystemMemory is a shared resource tracked on a single gauge from -10 to +10, starting at 0.Playing or digging with cards costs memory, moving the counter toward your opponent's side.If the memory counter passes 0 onto your opponent’s side (1 or higher), your turn ends immediately and your opponent takes over with the remaining memory.Combat and WinningDigivolving: Stack a higher-level Digimon on top of an eligible lower-level Digimon matching the color requirements by paying the digivolve cost.Attacking: Suspend your active Digimon to attack an opponent's suspended Digimon or the opponent's security stack.Security Checks: When attacking security, reveal the top security card and compare DP (Digimon Power); higher DP wins.
