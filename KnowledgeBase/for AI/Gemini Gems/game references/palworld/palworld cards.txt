Functional Card TypesPal Cards: Represent creatures from the game that you deploy to fight, harvest, and execute elemental attacks.Structure (Building) Cards: Placed on the field to provide ongoing utility, assign Pals, or produce resources.Gear Cards: Equipments or items attached to assist your Pals or enhance combat capabilities.Event Cards: Action or plot cards played to trigger specific sudden effects or tactical advantages.Soul Cards: Act as the resource or mana system housed in a separate Soul Deck to power your plays.All/Colorless/Multi-types: General categories or alternative classifications used for tracking deck colors (Red, Blue, Green, Purple).
