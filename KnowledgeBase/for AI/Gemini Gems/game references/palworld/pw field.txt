Main Deck Area: Where you place your full shuffled 50-card main deck. Running out of cards here results in a loss.Soul Deck Area: Where your separate 10-card resource deck (Soul Deck) is kept.Soul Area: Where active "Soul" resource cards are placed and rested to pay costs during your turn.Base (Battlefield): The central area where you deploy and rest your Pals, Gear, and Structure cards to perform actions and combat.Graveyard: The discard pile where defeated Pals and destroyed cards are sent.Exile Zone: A specialized zone where specific card effects send exiled cards instead of the graveyard.
