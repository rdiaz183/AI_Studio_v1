The Three Core Card TypesMonster Cards (Yellow/Orange/Various Colors): The main battling force with Attack (ATK) and Defense (DEF) values. They include Normal, Effect, and Extra Deck monsters.Spell Cards (Green): Magic cards played from your hand during your turn to boost monsters, draw cards, or disrupt the opponent. Subtypes include Normal, Quick-Play, Continuous, Equip, Field, and Ritual Spells.Trap Cards (Pink/Purple): Defensive cards set face-down on your field during your turn and activated during your opponent's turn. Subtypes include Normal, Continuous, and Counter Traps.Monster Subtypes & Special Summon MechanicsNormal & Effect Monsters: Normal monsters have flavor text and no special abilities. Effect monsters possess unique text abilities.Ritual Monsters (Blue): Summoned from the main deck by using a specific Ritual Spell and tributing monsters whose total levels match the Ritual Monster's level.Fusion Monsters (Purple): Kept in the Extra Deck and summoned by combining specific material monsters, traditionally using a card like Polymerization.Synchro Monsters (White): Extra Deck monsters summoned by matching a "Tuner" monster plus one or more non-Tuner monsters whose levels equal the Synchro Monster's level.Xyz Monsters (Black): Extra Deck monsters with "Ranks" instead of levels, summoned by stacking two or more monsters of the same level together as "materials."Pendulum Monsters (Half-Green/Half-Orange): Dual-purpose cards that can be played as monsters on the field or as spells in the Pendulum Zones to enable mass pendulum summons.Link Monsters (Dark Blue): Extra Deck monsters with no level or DEF points, summoned by sending face-up monsters on the field matching their specific link rating, featuring arrows that open up extra monster zones.
