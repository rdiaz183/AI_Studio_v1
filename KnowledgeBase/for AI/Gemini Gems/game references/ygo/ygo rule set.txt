Deck RulesMain Deck: Must have 40 to 60 cards.Extra Deck: Can have up to 15 special monster cards.Card Limit: You can only have up to three copies of the same card in your deck.Turn StructureEach turn goes through six phases:Draw Phase: Draw one card from your deck (the first player skips this on turn one).Standby Phase: Pay costs or activate specific card effects.Main Phase 1: Summon monsters, change positions, and play spells or traps.Battle Phase: Declare attacks with your monsters.Main Phase 2: Do more summons or set cards if you missed anything earlier.End Phase: Pass the turn to your opponent.Card TypesMonsters: Used to fight and defend. They have Attack (ATK) and Defense (DEF) stats. Levels are shown by stars. Level 5 and 6 monsters need one tribute (sacrificing a monster on your field). Level 7 or higher need two tributes.Spells: Green cards played on your turn to help you.Traps: Reddish cards set face down on your turn and activated during your opponent's turn.Winning the DuelYou win a duel by:Dropping your opponent's Life Points to 0.Making your opponent run out of cards to draw.Using a special card condition that says you win (like Exodia).Watch this quick visual breakdown of the turn structure and basic rules:
