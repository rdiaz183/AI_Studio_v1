Basic Setup and TurnsDeck Size: You must build a deck of exactly 30 cards using your chosen hero class.Starting Health: Both heroes begin the match with 30 health.Card Draw: A coin flip decides who goes first. The first player draws three cards, while the second player draws four cards.The Coin: The second player receives a special spell card called The Coin, which gives one extra mana crystal for a single turn.** Mulligan:** Both players can swap any number of cards from their starting hand for new ones from their deck before the first turn.Mana and ResourcesMana Crystals: You need mana crystals to play cards. Each card has a mana cost in its top-left corner.Mana Growth: You start your first turn with one max mana crystal. You gain one extra max mana crystal each turn up to a maximum of ten.Refilling Mana: All spent mana crystals refill at the start of your turn.Card Types and WinningMinions: Creatures that stay on the board and can attack enemy minions or the enemy hero.Spells: Magical actions that happen immediately when played.Weapons: Items equipped by your hero to let them attack directly.Win Condition: You win the game when the enemy hero's health reaches zero.
