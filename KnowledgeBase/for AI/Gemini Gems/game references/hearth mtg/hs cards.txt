Main Card TypesMinions: Creatures placed onto the battlefield to attack enemies and use special abilities. They feature an Attack value and Health value.Spells: One-time magical or physical actions that resolve immediately when played. Sub-types include Secrets, which trigger secretly during your opponent's turn.Weapons: Equipment for your hero that grants Attack power and Durability, allowing your hero to strike enemies directly.Hero Cards: Special cards that replace your current hero, upgrade or change your Hero Power, and usually grant armor.Locations: Persistent board fixtures with an ability that you can activate on your turn for a mana cost, usually with a cooldown before they can be used again.Core and Keyword MechanicsBattlecry: An effect that triggers immediately when you play the minion from your hand.Deathrattle: An effect that triggers when the minion dies.Taunt: Forces enemy minions to attack the Taunt minion instead of your other characters or your hero.Divine Shield: Absorbs the next instance of damage dealt to a minion.Stealth: Prevents a minion from being attacked or targeted by the enemy until it deals damage.Windfury: Allows a character to attack twice in a single turn.Discover: Lets you look at three random cards and choose one to add to your hand.Rush: Allows a minion to attack enemy minions the turn it is summoned, but it cannot attack the enemy hero immediately.Charge: Allows a minion to attack enemies (including the hero) the very turn it is summoned.Lifesteal: Heals your hero equal to the damage dealt by the card.Overload: Temporarily locks a number of your Mana crystals on your next turn in exchange for playing a powerful card ahead of curve.
