Core Card TypesLegend: The central leader card that defines your deck's strategy and domain pairing.Champion: Powerful character units representing iconic League of Legends champions that lead the charge on the board.Unit: Standard follower or minion cards played from your main deck to build board presence.Spell: Instant or action-speed effects used to deal damage, remove threats, or alter the game state.Rune: Resource cards stored in your rune deck to generate energy and power your plays.Gear: Equipment and items attached to units to provide stats like Might or special combat attributes.Battlefield: Location cards that set up the lanes where combat and point scoring take place.Token: Auxiliary cards generated during matches by specific card effects or abilities.
