Core Objective and WinningGoal: Be the first player to score 8 points and have strictly more points than any opponent.Scoring: Earn points by conquering empty or enemy-controlled battlefields and holding them until your next turn.The Final Point: The 8th point cannot be earned casually; you must either hold a battlefield or conquer two battlefields in the same turn. [1] (https://riftbound.gg/rules/), [2] (https://otakura.com/en/blogs/la-tana-del-nerd/how-to-play-riftbound-tcg), [3] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2koNsAKsipc&t=12)Deckbuilding RulesMain Deck: At least 40 cards (or 25+ for limited/sealed formats) built around a chosen Champion Legend.Rune Deck: Exactly 12 rune cards used to generate resources.Domain Colors: Cards must match the color identity (Fury, Calm, Mind, Body, Chaos, or Order) of your Champion Legend.Battlefields: Each player brings distinct battlefield cards to the table during setup. [1] (https://otakura.com/en/blogs/la-tana-del-nerd/how-to-play-riftbound-tcg), [2] (https://www.danireon.com/blogs/news/riftbound-deck-building-rules), [3] (https://ultimateguard.com/en/blog/riftbound-limited-rules-and-tricks-for-playing-sealed?srsltid=AfmBOopYMDR0XIEHrt3mewzq2VunBq6yp5zMXfMjvEAMIJEHZ7a10z11), [4] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sPvBt2mroDY&vl=en&t=7)Turn StructureEvery turn follows a specific order of phases: [1] (https://runesandrift.com/riftbound-turn-order/)Awaken Phase: Ready all your exhausted runes, units, and gear.Beginning Phase: Score points from holding battlefields and trigger start-of-turn effects.Channel Phase: Channel two runes from your rune deck into your base.Draw Phase: Draw one card and clear your rune pool.Main Phase: Play cards, deploy units, set gear, move toward battlefields, and initiate combat.End of Turn Phase: Clear temporary effects and pass the turn.
