Technical Rulebook & System Specification (v3.4 Patch - Summoning Sickness & Clock Overhaul)Aegis Breach is a high-tempo, 100% digital cross-platform trading card game designed for PC, iOS, and Android. Matches feature zero manual health/HP bookkeeping, automated mana growth, rank-gated deployment, and reactive off-turn decision windows. Matches are played in a Best of 3 format consisting of 3 matches per set.  Loss ConditionsA player instantly suffers a Match Defeat if either condition occurs:  Damage Defeat: Receiving a direct hit while holding 0 remaining cards in your Damage Zone.  Deckout Defeat: Being forced to draw a card when your Main Deck contains 0 cards.  Playmat Architecture & Zone LayoutThe playmat consists of a symmetric layout designed for visual clarity across mobile and PC displays. The Extra Deck rests on the field directly adjacent to the Grave, while the Side Deck remains entirely off-board during gameplay. Leader Zone (Back-Left): Houses the 4-card non-deck Leader Evolution Stack (Rank 0 through Rank 3).  Support Zone (Back-Center): Holds continuous Support cards granting board-wide passives or activated abilities.  Main Deck Zone (Back-Right): Holds the player’s exact 50-card deck.  Damage Zone (Front-Left): Holds 6 face-down cards set from the top of the deck at match start.  Vanguard Zone (Front-Center): Holds up to 5 active combat Units.  Grave & Extra Deck Zone (Front-Right): Receives destroyed units, spent items, resolved interrupts, and discarded guards. Houses the 15-card face-down Extra Deck adjacent to the Grave.  Side Deck (Off-Board): Contains up to 10 cards kept off the playmat for post-match adjustments during Best of 3 sets.  Deckbuilding, Extra/Side Mechanics & Pre-Game InitializationMain Deck: Exactly 50 cards. Maximum 3 copies of any card title.  Leader Stack: Exactly 4 Leader cards (Rank 0, Rank 1, Rank 2, Rank 3) kept in the Leader Zone.  Extra Deck: Exactly 15 cards kept adjacent to the Grave.  Side Deck: Exactly 10 cards kept off-board.  Hand Size Cap: 8-Card Hand Cap. Active players holding more than 8 cards must discard down to 8 cards during the Turn End Phase.  Side Decking & Extra Deck Swapping ProtocolBetween games in a Best of 3 match, players may adjust their decks using the Side Deck:  Side-to-Main Swapping: Players can swap cards between their Main Deck and Side Deck on a strict 1-for-1 basis (Main Deck size must remain exactly 50 cards).  Side-to-Extra Swapping: Players can swap cards from their Extra Deck by removing cards from their Extra Deck and replacing them with cards from their Side Deck on a 1-for-1 basis (Extra Deck size must remain exactly 15 cards).  Pre-Game Initialization & Mulligan ProcedureBoth players place their Rank 0 Leader face-up in the Leader Zone with Ranks 1–3 stacked face-down beneath it.  Both players place their 15-card Extra Deck face-down next to their Grave Zone.  Both players shuffle their 50-card Main Deck.  Place the top 6 cards of the Main Deck face-down into the Damage Zone.  Both players draw an initial starting hand of 5 cards.  Pre-Game Mulligan Phase: Before Turn 1 begins, both players simultaneously choose any number of cards (0 to 5) from their hand to return to the Main Deck. Each player shuffles their deck and draws back the exact number of returned cards. This mulligan occurs exactly once per match.  Color Identity, Stat Architecture & Dynamic ScalingColor System & Dual-Color Rules: Aegis Breach features seven core colors representing elemental themes. Colors reflect thematic affinity only and do not dictate or constrain strategic playstyles or deck archetypes.  Leader Alignment & Playability: Main Deck cards must share at least one color with your Leader's color identity. For instance, if your active Leader is Yellow, you can play pure Yellow cards, or if a card is dual-color (e.g., Yellow and Blue), it remains fully playable since it shares at least one matching color trait.  Dual-Color Cards & Client Indicators: Permitted in deckbuilding as long as at least one of their color traits matches your active Leader. Lightweight client-side visual badge indicators and cost-to-impact parameters appear dynamically in the card browser to assist pre-MVP deck composition.  Dynamic Cost-to-Impact Scaling (All Cards): Every card in the game scales its Mana cost directly proportional to its effect strength (Low-Impact = 1 Mana, Mid-Impact = 2 Mana, High-Impact Interruption/Board Shift = 3+ Mana). Turn Structure, Draw Phase & Mana ScalingUniversal Mana & Draw Rules: Starting Base Mana: Both players start Game Turn 1 with 2 Max Mana. Turn Mana Increment: Each player gains +1 Max Mana at the start of their turn (capped at 10 Mana).  Symmetrical Openings: Both Player 1 and Player 2 begin with 2 starting mana (gains +1 to play with 3 Mana) and draw 1 card during the Draw Phase. Summoning sickness applies equally, establishing balanced, uniform early-game conditions across aggressive and control archetypes.Subsequent Turns (Turn 2+): Both players gain +1 Max Mana at the start of their turn and draw 1 card during their Turn Phase.  Turn Phase ProgressionDraw Phase: Active player automatically draws 1 card from Main Deck.  Mana & Evolution Phase: Max Mana capacity increases by +1 (up to 10 Mana). Active player may advance their Leader by +1 Rank for 0 Mana.  Main Phase: Deploy units, place Support cards, or play Spells up to active Leader's Rank.  Combat Phase: Declare attacks against Vanguard units or opposing Leader (subject to Summoning Sickness).  End Phase: Hand size check. Discard down to 8 cards if holding excess. Unspent Mana carries over to fund off-turn responses during opponent's turn.  Combat Engine & Vanguard-Style Resolution FlowVanguard Taunt Rule: Opponents cannot declare direct attacks against your Leader while you control 1 or more units in your Vanguard Zone.  Tapping & Universal Intercept: Tapping (Resting): Rotates unit card horizontally to indicate an attack was declared. Universal Intercept: Any Vanguard unit can commit its printed Shield Value to defense, regardless of whether it is tapped or untapped.  Combat Resolution Threshold:$$\text{Total Defense} = \text{Target Base ATK} + \sum \text{Hand Guards} + \sum \text{Field Intercepts} + \sum \text{Spells}$$Combat resolution uses the threshold rule where ties favor the attacker:  Successful Hit ($\text{Attacker ATK} \ge \text{Total Defense}$): Unit Target: Defending unit is destroyed and sent to the Grave. Leader Target: Incurs 1 Direct Damage from the Damage Zone. Suffer Leader defeat penalties if applicable.  Blocked Attack ($\text{Attacker ATK} < \text{Total Defense}$): The attack fails. Defending unit survives, while all committed Hand Guards, Intercepts, and Spells enter the Grave.  Damage Shield Choice Resolution & Guard Break Tactical FrameworkWhen taking direct damage:  Calculated Seeding & Previewing: Players can utilize early-game support cards and Rank 0 deployments to look at, rearrange, or peek at the top card of their Damage Zone ahead of time, turning blind RNG into a calculated risk-reward system.  Conditional Resource Gating: The top card of the Damage Zone is revealed and added directly to the player's hand. If the card contains [GUARD BREAK], activating its effect requires committing a minor resource (paying 1 unspent Mana or exhausting an active unit) rather than triggering automatically for free.  Synergy Reward Loop: If the player successfully previewed or seeded that specific card using a support effect earlier in the match, the resource cost for the Guard Break is fully waived or grants an enhanced tactical bonus.  Failsafe Hand-Return: If the resource cost is not paid or the player lacks the required resource, the effect is suppressed, but the card safely remains in the hand to be deployed as a standard 15,000 Shield Hand Guard or Interceptor.  Traditional TCG Resolution & LIFO Stack ArchitectureActivations resolve on a Last-In, First-Out (LIFO) chain using standard, streamlined TCG mechanics (mirroring Yu-Gi-Oh! and Cardfight!! Vanguard):  No Re-Targeting Principle: If a card's primary target leaves the field or becomes illegal before chain resolution, the effect does not re-target and simply skips the invalid targeted interaction cleanly without complex backend overrides or guessing player intent.  Independent Clause Separation: Multi-part card text evaluates independent non-targeted actions (such as drawing cards, milling decks, or adjusting mana pools) separately, allowing utility clauses to complete successfully while strict targeted components require absolute card legality at resolution.  Cost Commitment & Resource Finality: Mana expenditures, card tappings, and material costs remain locked upon initial declaration without resource refunds, preserving the strict tactical risk of timing reactive interrupts.  Digital Master Duel Clock Engine & Timer OverhaulBase Time Pool: 7 minutes of total main thinking time allocated per player per match to protect competitive pacing.LIFO Loop Extension Removal: Permanently abolishes the dynamic +5-second time extension per stack link to eliminate timer-bleeding exploits by control archetypes during deep reactive loops.Structured Priority Windows: Sub-timer system where individual stack response links draw from a fixed, non-renewable time reserve.Response Cap: Maximum of 10 seconds allowed per priority pass on an individual LIFO stack response link.Automatic Resolution: Failure to respond within the 10-second sub-window automatically passes priority down the stack, preventing intentional stalling loops during complex counter-war phases.Penalty Framework: Implements an immediate soft-warning system for repetitive micro-pauses, escalating to a game-loss penalty if a competitor triggers three automated priority passes due to clock-running behavior within a single match round.Auto-Pass Filter: Automatically bypasses off-turn priority prompts if current Mana or hand resources cannot alter combat outcomes.
