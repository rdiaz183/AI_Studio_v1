### Sleep / Disable / Kill bodies

The Sleep and Disable fields bring rigid bodies to rest when their velocities go below a given threshold value. Higher values will stop objects more aggressively. Let's take a look at the details panel. You can select the activation of the field. You can also select the threshold field falloff shape, for example box, sphere or plane. Also, you can select the field behavior sleep, disable or kill. When you scroll down you will have display option very similar to the anchor field. So for example you can change the color of your field. As well you can select if you want this field to be a chaos field. Actually this field are used to improve simulation performance and prevent excess jittering of the geometry collection pieces as they collide with each other. Sleeping bodies can be reawakened when they collide with an active object. Disabled bodies are removed from the simulation completely after they come to a resting state. Disabled bodies cannot be reawakened. Kill option ignores the threshold value and immediately stops the body from simulating and remove it from simulation once it overlaps the field's volume. The results can be subtle, but it offers better performance and optimization for your simulation. If you take a look at the frames per second information, you can see the difference by using the disable, kill and sleeping rigid bodies. Okay, I'll see you in the next lecture.
