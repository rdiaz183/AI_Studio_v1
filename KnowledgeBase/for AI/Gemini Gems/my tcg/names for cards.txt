### Catchy Naming Alternatives & Progression Architecture

Here are three distinct, catchy naming direction themes for your time-traveling Toku TCG. Each theme clearly maps out how a squad member evolves from **Rank 1 (Pre-Ranger / Civilian-Trainee)** into **Rank 2 (Era Ranger / Suited Form)**, while keeping the MC as the flexible 7th Ranger.

---

### Option 1: The "Epoch-Shift" Theme (Sleek, High-Energy)

*Focuses on high-octane gear shifts and time jumps. Fits a fast-paced, modern action vibe.*

* **7th Ranger MC:** **Cipher** (The unwritten timeline code)
* *Rank 0:* Cadet Cipher
* *Rank 1:* Cipher: Zero-Suit (Pre-Ranger / Tactical Training Armor)
* *Rank 2:* Cipher: Era Prime (First Morphed Form)
* *Rank 3:* Cipher: Apex Overdrive (Heavy Exo-Frame)


* **Main Squad (Set 1 - Base Timeline Era):**
* *Rank 1 (Pre-Ranger):* Shift Initiate: [Color/Name] *(e.g., Shift Initiate: Crimson)*
* *Rank 2 (Era Ranger):* Epoch Ranger: [Color] *(e.g., Epoch Ranger: Crimson)*


* **Collectible Key Item:** **Epoch Keys**
* **Morpher Spell:** **Shift-Drive: Ignition**

* *Rank 1:* Flux: Anchor Mode (Pre-Ranger Prototype)
* *Rank 2:* Flux: Continuum Ranger (Full Era Suit)
* *Rank 3:* Flux: Sovereign Zenith (Boss Form)


* **Main Squad (Set 1 - Base Timeline Era):**
