# OFFICIAL MASTER RULEBOOK

---

## 1. System Overview & Victory Condition

### 1.1 Objective

The objective of the game is to dismantle your opponent’s defenses and land a final direct hit against them when their Damage Zone is completely depleted.

### 1.2 Victory Conditions

A player instantly wins the match when any of the following conditions are met:

* **Direct Elimination:** You declare a successful direct attack against an opposing player who has **0 cards remaining** in their Damage Zone.
* **Deck Out:** An opponent is required to draw a card from their Main Deck during any phase but has no cards remaining in their Main Deck.

---

## 2. Deck Construction Guidelines

Every player must bring two separate decks to a match: a **Main Deck** and a **Commander Stack**.

| Deck Type | Exact Card Count | Restrictions & Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Main Deck** | 50 Cards | Contains Units, Spells, Items, and Tactics. Maximum 4 copies of any card with the same name. Governed strictly by Color Identity rules. |
| **Commander Stack** | 4 Cards | Contains exactly 1 Rank 0, 1 Rank 1, 1 Rank 2, and 1 Rank 3 Commander card belonging to the same Commander line. Kept in the Commander Deck Zone. |

---

## 3. Field Architecture & Play Zones

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                      SHARED MEMORY GAUGE                                           |
|  [-10]  [-8]  [-6]  [-4]  [-2]  | 0 |  [+2]  [+4]  [+6]  [+8]  [+10]                             |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [DAMAGE ZONE]        [SUPPORT 1]        [SUPPORT 2]        [SUPPORT 3]        [MAIN DECK]         |
|  (6 Setup Cards)      (Back Row)         (Back Row)         (Back Row)         (50 Cards)          |
|                                                                                                    |
|  [BREEDING /          [VANGUARD 1]       [VANGUARD 2]       [VANGUARD 3]       [GRAVEYARD]         |
|   COMMANDER ZONE]     (Front Row)        (Front Row)        (Front Row)        (Discard / Drop)    |
|  (Safe Progression)                                                                                |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

```

### 3.1 Zone Definitions

* **Shared Memory Gauge:** Centered between both players. Tracks shared turn energy from -10 to +10.
* **Damage Zone (Top-Left):** Stores 6 setup cards representing player life.
* **Breeding / Commander Zone (Bottom-Left):** Protected zone where your active Commander resides and evolves safely.
* **Vanguard Lanes (Front Row - Center):** Holds up to 3 active combat units. Vanguard units form the defensive wall protecting the player.
* **Support Lanes (Back Row - Center):** Holds up to 3 utility units. Back Row units provide passive abilities, aura buffs, and resource manipulation. They cannot declare standard physical attacks nor be targeted by standard physical strikes.
* **Main Deck Zone (Top-Right):** Houses your 50-card Main Deck face-down.
* **Graveyard (Bottom-Right):** Face-up discard pile for destroyed units, spent Spells, discarded cards, and removed Equipment.

---

## 4. Game Setup Sequence

1. **Place Commander Stack:** Place your 4-card Commander Stack face-down in the Breeding/Commander Zone. Reveal the top card (**Rank 0 Commander**).
2. **Build Damage Zone:** Shuffle your 50-card Main Deck. Take the top 6 cards without looking at them and place them face-down into your Damage Zone.
3. **Draw Opening Hand:** Both players draw **7 cards** from their Main Deck.
4. **Mulligan Phase:**
* Each player may choose any number of cards from their hand and return them to the bottom of their Main Deck in any order.
* Draw an equal number of cards to replace them.
* Shuffle the Main Deck thoroughly.


5. **Set Shared Memory:** Place the Memory Gauge marker at **0**. Flip a coin or roll a die; the winner chooses who takes the first turn.

---

## 5. Resource Engine: Shared Memory Gauge

```text
[Opponent's Side]  <- (-10) ... (-1) |  0  | (+1) ... (+10) ->  [Your Side]

```

### 5.1 Memory Flow

* All card costs and effect activations consume Memory, sliding the marker toward your opponent's side by an amount equal to the cost paid.
* Your turn ends **immediately** when an action pushes the Memory marker past `0` onto your opponent's side of the scale.
* Your opponent begins their turn with whatever excess Memory you passed to them (e.g., if you push the gauge to `-3`, your opponent begins their turn with `+3` Memory).

### 5.2 The Turn Floor & Setter Rules

* **The Pass Floor:** If a player passes their turn while the gauge is at `0`, the marker automatically moves to `3` on the opponent's side, guaranteeing a minimum of 3 Memory.
* **Memory Setters:** If a player begins their turn with 2 or less Memory, any active "Memory Setter" effects immediately adjust their starting Memory gauge floor to `3`.

---

## 6. Turn Structure

Turn priority cycles through five sequential phases:

```text
1. STAND PHASE ──> 2. DRAW PHASE ──> 3. RIDE PHASE ──> 4. MAIN PHASE ──> 5. END PHASE

```

### 6.1 Stand Phase

Ready (turn upright) all Rested units on your field.

### 6.2 Draw Phase

Draw 1 card from your Main Deck. *(The player going first draws on Turn 1).*

### 6.3 Ride Phase

Once per turn, take the next higher Rank card directly from your Commander Stack (Rank 0 $\rightarrow$ Rank 1 $\rightarrow$ Rank 2 $\rightarrow$ Rank 3) and stack it on top of your current Commander in the Breeding Zone.

### 6.4 Main Phase

You may perform any of the following actions in any order as long as you possess available Memory:

* **Call Units:** Call units from your hand to open Vanguard or Support slots (**Cost: 0 Memory**). *Restriction: You cannot call a unit whose Rank is higher than your Commander's current active Rank.*
* **Play Spells & Items:** Pay the indicated Memory cost to execute Spell effects or attach Items.
* **Activate Effects:** Pay Memory costs to trigger unit or Commander abilities.
* **Declare Combat:** Rest active Vanguard units to declare attacks against enemy Vanguard units or the opposing Player.

### 6.5 End Phase

Triggers automatically the exact moment the Memory Gauge marker crosses `0` onto your opponent's side. Unresolved actions terminate, and turn priority shifts to your opponent.

---

## 7. Equipment & The Equip Keyword

### 7.1 Attachment Process

Cards featuring the **[EQUIP]** keyword are played during the Main Phase by paying their Memory cost and placing them **underneath** a targeted Unit in a Vanguard or Support slot.

### 7.2 Inherited Traits & Stats

* The top Unit gains all ATK/DEF bonuses and passive abilities printed on cards equipped underneath it.
* A Unit may have multiple cards equipped under it unless explicitly restricted by card text.

### 7.3 Discarding Equipment

If an equipped Unit is KO'd, destroyed, or returned to the Breeding Zone/hand, all cards tucked underneath it are detached and sent directly to the Graveyard.

---

## 8. Combat & Guarding Architecture

### 8.1 The Vanguard Frontline Wall

* **Player Protection:** You **cannot** attack the opposing Player directly while *any* unit occupies an enemy Vanguard slot (Vanguard 1, 2, or 3).
* **Clear Frontline Condition:** All 3 enemy Vanguard slots must be completely empty before a direct attack against the Player can be declared.
* **Universal Vanguard Targeting:** Attacking units may target **any** unit standing in an enemy Vanguard slot, regardless of whether that target is active (upright) or Rested (sideways).
* **Backline Protection:** Support units and Commanders in the Breeding Zone cannot be targeted by standard physical attacks.

### 8.2 Combat Resolution Step-by-Step

```text
[1. Declare Attacker & Target] ──> Turn attacker Rested (sideways).
             │
             ▼
[2. Guard Step (Defender)]     ──> Pitch cards from hand to Guard Circle for +Shield DEF.
             │
             ▼
[3. Damage Comparison]         ──> Attacker ATK vs. (Defender ATK + Total Shield).
             │
             ├───────────────────────────────────┐
             ▼                                   ▼
  [Attacker ATK >= Defender]          [Attacker ATK < Defender]
             │                                   │
             ▼                                   ▼
Defending Unit is KO'd/Destroyed.     Defender Survives. No Damage.

```

### 8.3 Commander KO Recovery Rule

If your Commander is moved to a Vanguard slot and KO'd in combat:

1. It is **not** sent to the Graveyard.
2. Return it directly to your **Breeding / Commander Zone**.
3. It enters a suppressed state: **All of its card effects and passive abilities are negated for the remainder of that turn.**

---

## 9. Damage, Break Triggers & Overdrive

### 9.1 Damage Resolution (Direct Hit)

When an unblocked attack lands directly on a Player:

1. Select 1 face-down card from your **Damage Zone** and reveal it.
2. **Break Check:** If the card features a **Break** keyword, resolve its effect immediately for **FREE** as an instant-speed response.
3. **Hand Addition:** Add the revealed card directly to your **Hand**.

### 9.2 Overdrive Mechanics

Cards featuring **[OVERDRIVE]** text possess game-changing comeback abilities.

* **Activation Condition:** Overdrive effects can **only** be activated if you have **3 or fewer cards remaining** in your Damage Zone (meaning you have sustained 3 or more damage).

## 10. Deck Building & Color Identity

```text
                         [7 ELEMENTAL FACTIONS]
  Red (Fire)    Blue (Water)    Pink (Wind)    Green (Earth)    Yellow (Light)    Purple (Dark)
                                 Neutral (Colorless)

```

### 10.1 The Inclusive Color Matching Rule

Every non-Neutral card in your 50-card Main Deck must share **at least one color** with your designated Commander.

* **Mono-Color Commander (e.g., Pure Red):**
* **LEGAL:** Single-color Red cards, Dual-color cards containing Red (e.g., Red/Purple, Red/Yellow), and Neutral cards.
* **ILLEGAL:** Pure Purple, Blue/Green, or any card lacking Red identity.


* **Dual-Color Commander (e.g., Red/Blue):**
* **LEGAL:** Single-color Red, single-color Blue, Dual-color cards containing Red OR Blue (e.g., Red/Purple, Blue/Green), and Neutral cards.



### 10.2 Neutral (Colorless) Cards

Neutral cards carry no color identity and can be placed in any deck regardless of Commander color.

### 10.3 Archetype-Locked Neutral Commanders (The Prism Exception)

Specific Neutral Commanders (such as Prism / Squad Commanders) feature a **Golden Rule Deckbuilding Clause** that overrides standard color restrictions:

```text
[COMMANDER RULE: PRISM SQUAD]
Your deck may include cards of ANY color, provided every colored card 
contains the archetype name "[ARCHETYPE NAME]" in its card title. 
All non-archetype cards in your main deck must be Neutral (Colorless).

```

## 11. Quick Reference Keyword Index

* **Call:** Placing a unit from hand into a Vanguard or Support slot.
* **Rest:** Turning a card sideways to signify it has attacked or used an ability.
* **Stand:** Turning a Rested card upright to an active state.
* **Shield:** Bonus defense value gained by pitching a card from hand during the Guard Step.
* **Equip:** Placing an Item card underneath a unit to grant inherited stats and abilities.
* **Break:** Trigger keyword on Damage Zone cards allowing free, instant-speed casting when revealed from damage.
* **Overdrive:** High-potency abilities unlocked only when remaining Damage Zone cards $\le 3$.
